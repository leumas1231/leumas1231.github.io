As part of UW–Stevens Point’s Case In Point business competition, my team developed a UX-focused strategy to help Noble Community Clinics attract and retain dental professionals in rural communities. We applied user research, competitive analysis, usability testing, and wireframing to redesign Noble’s careers experience. Our solution earned first place in the competition.

## Problem

Noble Community Clinics faced challenges attracting dental professionals to rural areas. Through initial stakeholder meetings, we identified that:

- The careers page lacked strong employee-focused messaging

- The website emphasized patient information over employee experience

- The brand did not clearly communicate workplace culture

- Rural relocation concerns (community, family, belonging) were not addressed

- The goal was to redesign the careers experience to make potential employees feel welcomed and connected before they even relocated.

## Process & Impact

Research & Stakeholder Interviews

- Met with the Noble leadership team to gather goals and challenges

- Identified key priorities: culture, mission, employee relationships, social media presence

- Shifted perspective from a client-focused site to a people-focused recruitment strategy

Competitive Analysis

- Evaluated competitor career pages

- Identified strengths such as community storytelling, culture highlights, and clear calls to action

- Noted gaps in Noble’s presentation compared to certified employers

Usability Testing

Conducted usability testing with participants across multiple age groups and professional backgrounds:

- 25-year-old shift lead

- 60-year-old IT systems administrator

- 20-year-old college student

Key findings:

- Users were unsure what type of clinic Noble was

- The work environment felt unclear

- Career growth information was limited

- The page felt informational but not inviting

Wireframing & Design (Adobe XD)

- Developed a redesigned careers page mockup

- Prioritized culture, growth, and community integration

- Simplified layout and clarified information hierarchy

- Highlighted social media presence and employee experience

- Integrated community partnerships and relocation support information

## Results

- Delivered a complete UX redesign proposal

- Presented strategic implementation plan

- Proposed social media and certification strategy to improve employer branding

- Earned first place in the Case In Point competition

## Reflection

This experience strengthened my ability to approach UX from both a user and strategic perspective. I learned how recruitment experiences differ from traditional consumer-facing UX and how storytelling, community integration, and clear messaging influence decision-making. Collaborating in a multidisciplinary team environment reinforced the importance of research, iteration, and alignment with stakeholder goals.

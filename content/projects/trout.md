A UX-focused volunteer project to improve the usability, navigation, and accessibility of the Fox Valley Trout Unlimited website. The goal was to help visitors quickly find key information such as events, membership details, and conservation initiatives on both desktop and mobile devices.


Fox Valley Trout Unlimited is an active chapter of
Trout Unlimited located in the Fox Valley
southwest of Green Bay Wisconsin.

<a href="https://foxvalleytu.org/" target="_blank">Trout Unlimited's Main Page</a>

## Responsibilities

- Developed a strategic approach to enhance the organization’s online presence, which improved engagement with
members and increased visibility within the community.
- Reorganized and restructured WordPress website to better align with organization’s goals, enhancing site navigation,
user experience, and ensuring mobile responsiveness for better accessibility on all devices.
- Produced engaging video content to effectively communicate organization's mission and events.
- Integrated Facebook feed into website to streamline event promotion, leading to more efficient event management
and improved participation from members.
- Worked closely with organization leaders ensuring digital strategies were in line with their vision, creating a more
unified and effective online presence.

## Problem

Users struggled to find priority content due to inconsistent layout, overwhelming navigation choices, and unclear content hierarchy. This created confusion around tasks like finding upcoming events, joining the organization, or learning about local initiatives.

## Process

- Evaluated existing site structure to understand common navigation paths

- Conducted informal stakeholder interviews to define user priorities

- Mapped user task flows to identify friction points

- Conducted informal usability testing by walking through common user tasks and gathering stakeholder feedback to identify navigation friction, unclear content hierarchy, and areas of cognitive overload.

- Simplified navigation by reducing clutter and prioritizing commonly accessed sections

- Reorganized content hierarchy to highlight events and membership information

- Tested layout responsiveness across mobile and tablet devices

## Design Decisions

- Consolidated navigation items into clearer, task-centered categories

- Emphasized key calls to action such as “Upcoming Events” and “Get Involved”

- Improved spacing and typography for better scanability

- Adjusted layouts for consistent behavior between desktop and mobile views

- Applied accessibility-aware design practices such as clear headings and consistent labels

## Impact

- Navigation became easier to follow, reducing cognitive load

- Key content (events, membership) became more prominent and easier to access

- Site visuals and layout felt more consistent and cohesive

- Stakeholders reported improved clarity and reduced visitor confusion

- Mobile usability increased through responsive adjustments

## Reflection

This project reinforced the value of user-centered design and thoughtful information architecture. I learned how subtle adjustments to layout and navigation can significantly improve usability—especially for diverse audiences accessing the site on multiple devices.

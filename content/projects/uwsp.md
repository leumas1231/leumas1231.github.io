As Web and Multimedia Coordinator for University Dining, I focus on improving usability, accessibility, and content clarity across a high-traffic campus website. The site serves students, faculty, and visitors who rely on quick access to menus, hours, and event information.

My work centers on translating stakeholder requirements into user-friendly layouts while maintaining consistency across pages and improving accessibility standards.

https://www.uwsp.edu/dining/university-dining-services/

## Responsibilities

- Build, maintain, and optimize front-end website content within WordPress using HTML and CSS ensuring responsive, cross-browser, and cross-device compatibility.
- Improve site navigation, accessibility, and mobile responsiveness, enhancing usability for a diverse campus audience.
- Collaborate with marketing teams and campus partners in a team-based environment to identify improvements in UX.
- Develop and publish web pages and digital promotions while maintaining consistent UI patterns and branding standards.
- Continuously evaluate and refine interfaces to improve clarity, performance, and user experience.

## Reflection

Working on a live campus site reinforced the importance of balancing stakeholder requirements with user needs. I learned that small layout and hierarchy changes can significantly impact clarity and usability, particularly for mobile users.
